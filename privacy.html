PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, your device.

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective: May 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

YATL is private by default. Your tasks, voice input, and daily activity never leave your device. The only personal data we ever collect is your email address — and only if you choose to give it to us for product updates. We do not sell data. We do not run ads. We do not track you.

Who we are

YATL is developed by Olu-Femi Salami, trading as Artizani. Questions about this policy can be directed to artizany@proton.me.

What we collect

1. Email address (optional)

If you choose to receive product updates, we collect your email address.

  • Used only for YATL product update emails
  • Never sold or shared with third parties
  • Stored with Kit.com — see kit.com/privacy
  • Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Legal basis (GDPR): Consent — you opt in voluntarily, never automatically

To remove your email: click unsubscribe in any email we send, or email artizany@proton.me.


2. Subscription status

If you subscribe to YATL Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your subscription status. They do not receive your payment details — those go directly to Apple.

See revenuecat.com/privacy.


We do NOT collect: your tasks or notes · your voice input · your location · usage analytics · advertising identifiers · contacts or calendar data · any data processed by speech recognition or AI (both run on-device only).

Data storage and security

  • All app data (tasks, settings, archives) is stored locally on your device
  • Nothing is synced to our servers — we don't have any
  • Reminders sync writes to the Apple Reminders app on your device only
  • Email addresses are stored securely with Kit.com using industry-standard encryption

Your rights

EU/UK users (GDPR) — You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California users (CCPA) — We do not sell personal information. You have the right to know what we collect and to request deletion.

To exercise any right, email artizany@proton.me. We respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

YATL is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided their email, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top of this page if anything changes. If you have provided your email, we will notify you of material changes.
