WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

voice brain dump

Speak your thoughts naturally. YATL uses on-device AI to structure them into an ordered, prioritised task list instantly.

daily reset

Every morning starts fresh. Completed tasks are archived as a text file. Your backlog cannot haunt you here.

zen mode

Full black screen. Just the time and your task name. Everything else disappears until the timer ends.

focus lock

Optionally block distracting apps for the duration of a session. One tap. Lifts automatically when you're done.

private by default

Voice recognition and AI parsing run entirely on your device. No data leaves your phone. No account required.

reminders sync

Your tasks appear in the native Reminders app automatically, so they're front and centre wherever you look.
