COMMON QUESTIONS

My tasks disappeared after midnight — is that a bug?

No, that's by design. YATL resets every morning to keep you focused on today only. Your completed tasks are archived as a .txt file you can download from the morning reset screen.

The microphone isn't working. What do I do?

Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → make sure YATL is switched on. Also check Speech Recognition is enabled the same way. Both are required for voice input.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → YATL → Cancel Subscription. YATL doesn't manage billing directly — it's all handled through Apple so you have full control.

I subscribed but Pro features aren't unlocking.

Open YATL → Settings → Restore Purchases. This syncs your subscription status with Apple. If it still doesn't work, email us at artizany@proton.me with your Apple ID and we'll sort it out.

Is my voice data private?

Yes. YATL uses on-device speech recognition with requiresOnDeviceRecognition enabled. Your voice never leaves your phone. The AI that organises your tasks also runs entirely on-device using Apple's Foundation Models.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID to request one.

The app blocking feature isn't available — why?

Focus Lock (app blocking) requires Screen Time permission, which you can grant the first time you tap the feature in the Focus tab. If you don't see the option, make sure you're on YATL Pro.
