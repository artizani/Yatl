YET ANOTHER TODO LIST

"Your todo list is not the problem. Your relationship with it is."

YATL was built on a simple belief: the best productivity system is one you actually use. Every day. Without guilt.

THE STORY

Most todo apps are built for capture. They're excellent at helping you collect tasks, organise them into projects, assign priorities, set due dates, and build an ever-growing backlog of things you haven't done yet.

The problem is the backlog. It grows. It follows you. Every morning you open the app and see yesterday's guilt waiting for you. The system that was supposed to reduce stress becomes the source of it.

YATL is built for execution. You speak your day, it structures your thoughts, you work through them one at a time, and at midnight everything resets. Completed tasks are archived quietly. Unfinished ones disappear. Tomorrow starts clean.

It's not for everyone. But for the people it's for, nothing else works quite like it.

App description (long)

YATL (Yet Another Todo List) is a focus and daily execution app that replaces the anxiety of a growing backlog with the simplicity of a single focused day. Voice input powered by on-device AI organises your brain dump into a prioritised task list. A focus timer keeps you on track. Zen mode removes all distractions. Everything resets at midnight.
